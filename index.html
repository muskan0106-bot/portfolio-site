What is Aestera Living & What we are Offering

Design With Clarity.

Aestera Living is a modern Online Interior Design platform created to simplify how homes are planned and designed. Instead of complicated processes or contractor-driven decisions, we focus on clear design planning that helps homeowners execute their interiors confidently. Our goal is simple: help you understand your space better before construction or carpentry begins.

With structured layouts, practical documentation, and thoughtful design guidance, Aestera Living makes interior planning more transparent and accessible. No contractor lock-ins. No product pushing. Just thoughtful design planning that works.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I’m Muskan Shrivastav, an Interior designer with over 5 years of experience and the founder of Aestera Living.

I started Aestera Living with a simple belief "Design should feel clear, intentional, and aligned with the way you live". Over the years, I realized that homeowners today don’t lack ideas, they often lack clarity in turning those ideas into a well-planned space.

Through Aestera Living, I work closely with clients to simplify the design process through thoughtful planning, clear direction, and a more personal, collaborative approach to interiors. Every project is founder-led, with a strong focus on creating spaces that feel functional, effortless, and truly reflective of the people living in them.

My approach is rooted in transparency, attention to detail, and designing homes that not only look beautiful, but also feel practical, comfortable, and deeply personal to everyday living.
